Cat Codex is a free, fan-made companion for The Battle Cats that brings everything players usually hunt for across wikis and guides into one fast, easy app. It works on phones and computers, in light or dark mode. Every number comes straight from the game’s own data files (English version 15.6), not copied by hand.

Cats. Every unit in the English game is here: all 756 cats and 1,943 forms. That’s 10 Normal, 149 Special, 162 Rare, 103 Super Rare, 315 Uber Rare and 17 Legend Rare cats, including 409 True forms and 22 Ultra forms. Search by any form’s name or by unit number. Filter by rarity, the enemy types a cat targets, its abilities, its immunities and its attack type, then sort ten different ways, from highest DPS to cheapest cost. Open any cat to flip between its forms, drag its level from 1 up to its maximum with plus levels, and toggle full treasures. You’ll see health, damage, real DPS, range, attack rate, cost, recharge, speed and knockbacks, along with every ability in plain English, its evolution recipe, its level limits and its talents.

Catfruit. All 29 evolution items in one place: every Catfruit, seed, Behemoth Stone and Gem. Each one shows what it’s for, where to farm it, and every cat that needs it, with the total you’d need to evolve them all.

Leveling guide. How cats get stronger: XP levels, Catseyes for levels 31 to 60, plus levels from duplicates, level limits for every rarity, True and Ultra forms, talents and treasures, with worked examples.

Traits & abilities. What every enemy type is and what every ability actually does, from Massive damage and Freeze to Surge, Barrier breaker and Sage slayer.

Basics. A plain-language guide for new and returning players covering:

XP, Cat Food and User Rank, with a worked example showing exactly how your rank is added up.
Capsule odds, with a calculator for your real chance of pulling any Uber or one specific Uber.
The full story map, from Empire of Cats through The Aku Realms.
Stories of Legend, Uncanny Legends and Zero Legends.
Crowns and difficulty stars, with an interactive demo explaining pink stars.
Special Stages, the Catclaw Dojo, and a quick glossary.

Team builder. Build a 10-cat lineup just like in the game, with every cat shown fully maxed. Hover over any cat to see its full stats, which enemies it destroys fast (with its DPS against them), which it holds back, and what it’s immune to. Drag cats to rearrange them, lock your favorites, and check your team’s coverage against every enemy type.

Team maker. Tell it what a stage throws at you: enemy types, attacks, shields, barriers, or a single boss versus lots of enemies. It builds a balanced team with meatshields and counters, explains why it picked each cat, and grades your lineup with a stage checklist. Mark the cats you own and it will build only from those. Your team and your marks save on your device.
